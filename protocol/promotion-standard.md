# Candidate promotion standard

## Hard gates

A candidate is not eligible for the final three unless all are true:

- The research question is answerable in one clean sentence.
- At least two independent searches of recent primary literature fail to reveal an existing paper with the same central contribution.
- The candidate has explicit baselines and a falsifiable primary claim.
- Data and model access are lawful, public, or affordable.
- The main result remains useful if the preferred hypothesis is false.
- One independent researcher can implement and verify the core study.
- The study can be reproduced without undisclosed proprietary data or frontier-scale training.
- It is research rather than a product demo, prompt collection, or profession-specific ChatGPT application.

## Scored criteria

Score each item from 0 (fails) to 4 (strong), for a maximum of 60.

| Criterion | What a 4 requires |
| --- | --- |
| Verifiable literature gap | Closest work mapped; difference is substantive and testable |
| Question clarity | Clean estimand or evaluation target with bounded claims |
| Contribution compression | Contribution is credible in one or two sentences |
| Feasibility | Data, models, and engineering fit weeks rather than years |
| Reproducibility | Deterministic pipeline where possible; complete version and prompt logs |
| Negative-result value | Null or failure result resolves real uncertainty |
| Baseline quality | Simple and strong baselines are available and affordable |
| Internal validity | Confounds, leakage, contamination, and multiplicity are controllable |
| Technical fit | Achievable with current skills plus bounded skill development |
| Audience | Identifiable community has reason to care |
| Venue path | Honest working-paper, artifact, workshop, or conference route exists |
| Durability | Question is unlikely to become obsolete before completion |
| Follow-up potential | Natural second project follows from either outcome |
| Identity fit | Supports a coherent research program without relying on professional novelty |
| Independence | Not startup marketing and not dependent on privileged company data |

## Promotion rule

- No hard-gate failures.
- At least 45/60 overall.
- At least 3/4 for literature gap, internal validity, reproducibility, and feasibility.
- Top-three candidates receive independent literature, design, statistical, implementation, reproducibility, venue-fit, and red-team audits.
- Final selection occurs only after an explicit attempt to reject each finalist.

Scores organize judgment; they do not substitute for it.
