# Research notes

Every factual claim that affects project selection should trace to a primary source or the original paper. Secondary sources may help discover material but should not be the final evidence for novelty or method claims.

## Candidate evidence states

- `lead` — plausible question; novelty not checked
- `screened` — closest work identified; obvious duplicates removed
- `audited` — adversarial novelty and feasibility review completed
- `promoted` — satisfies the promotion standard and has an approved protocol
- `rejected` — retained with a concrete rejection reason

## Required candidate fields

Each candidate must state:

1. precise research question;
2. smallest meaningful contribution;
3. audience and why it matters;
4. closest papers and exact gap;
5. novelty condition;
6. required data, models, compute, cost, skills, and collaboration;
7. technical difficulty and realistic timeline;
8. likely format and venues;
9. useful negative outcome;
10. strongest publication risk.
