# Follow-up research roadmap

**Date:** 2026-07-18

## Research identity

The most credible continuing identity is **empirical reliability and measurement of AI decision systems under institutional constraints**. The comparative advantage is not legal commentary. It is the ability to turn rules, evidence burdens, procedure, and audit trails into executable tasks, explicit estimands, falsification criteria, and inspectable records.

The current methods/resource note is paper zero: evidence that the research process can stop responsibly. The next paper must establish a new fact under an identified design.

## Project 1 - identified context-history study

**Question:** For the same complete target, which separable features of prior independent cases cause excess decision error beyond generic context load, and does a boundary operation remove that effect?

**Minimum design:** multiple independent rule schemas; the same frozen targets crossed with fresh context, token/format-matched neutral history, case-only history, truthful label history, and at least one boundary operation; a common exposure bank where feasible; predeclared snapshot-specific admissibility otherwise; crossed target/history/schema effects; a formal heterogeneity criterion; and a competent panel or sharply fixed-snapshot estimand.

**Promotion gates:** no outcome-sensitive prompt repair; baseline task validity away from floor and ceiling; native-template matching without treatment padding; simulation of the actual crossed analysis; and an approved, priced call manifest before hosted inference.

**Useful negative result:** tight evidence that identified excess error is below a predeclared practical threshold in named snapshots, or evidence that admissibility itself is too snapshot-specific for a pooled cross-model estimand.

**Stop condition:** no competent panel, no common exposure construction, or no valid simultaneous/hierarchical procedure means the project remains a design study and does not proceed to scale.

## Project 2 - procedural recovery and enforcement

**Question:** When an AI decision agent violates an explicit institutional rule, which recovery signal changes subsequent behavior: a natural-language reminder, an execution counterexample, state reset, or formal enforcement that prevents the invalid action?

Build on the first project's rule worlds but add tool use and logged state transitions. Compare prompt-only recovery with a deterministic policy checker or constrained action interface. The central contribution must be a causal comparison of recovery mechanisms under matched tasks and budgets, not another prompt collection.

**Needed skills:** agent-harness engineering, event schemas, fault injection, survival/time-to-recovery analysis, and containerized execution.

**Likely audience:** TMLR, AAMAS, or an agents/evaluation workshop first; a broader venue requires replication across materially different agent tasks.

## Project 3 - evaluation validity across harnesses

**Question:** Which behavioral conclusions survive changes in orchestration, native templates, retry policies, and structured-output enforcement?

Use the current artifact-authentication and failure-preservation machinery to compare model-harness configurations on a frozen task bank. Predeclare the conclusion being tested, treat the harness as part of the evaluated object, and require a result that changes a ranking, uncertainty set, or deployment/evaluation decision. Engineering a portable runner is not enough by itself.

This project connects the institutional-decision niche to a more domain-neutral identity in evaluation measurement and reproducible agent science.

## Alternative pivot

If a competent context-history panel remains infeasible, pivot to the finalist F2 line only with collaboration: repeated complete draws from at least two established dynamic benchmark generators, independent validity ground truth, and empirical coverage analysis over mapped sampling hierarchies. A one-generator cluster bootstrap is not a paper. The contingency C06 forensic audit is also viable as a bounded reproduction project, but only if a clean trace-complete rerun recovers an interpretable estimate and shows how parser/fallback policy changes a scientific conclusion.

## Skill-development plan

Prioritize mixed-effects and hierarchical modeling, causal identification, simulation-based design, measurement validity, agent-state instrumentation, and release engineering. Seek collaborators for statistical design or domain validation only where they perform substantial accountable work. The clearest long-run signal will be a sequence of two or three related, reproducible studies that become more identified and externally tested over time.
