# AI assistance disclosure

Generative AI tools materially assisted with literature discovery, candidate ideation, protocol drafting, software and test scaffolding, debugging, analysis drafting, adversarial review, and manuscript editing.

No AI system is an author. The human author selected the research question, authorized the work, reviewed the claim-to-artifact links, accepted or rejected proposed changes, and remains responsible for the scientific judgments and public release.

AI-generated suggestions were treated as drafts rather than evidence. Load-bearing claims were checked against primary sources; executable behavior was checked with deterministic solvers and tests; exact prompts and raw model responses were retained; invalid runs were preserved; and the final report adopts the limitations found during independent reanalysis and adversarial review.

The language models used as experimental subjects are documented separately in the run manifests by repository, revision, runtime, and local artifact hashes. Model weights are not included in this repository.
