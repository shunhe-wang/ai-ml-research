## Approval boundary

Any hosted inference, cloud job, paid data, external annotation, publication fee, or subscription requires a priced plan and explicit approval before purchase. The stopped confirmatory design has no authorized budget. If it is ever reopened, cost must be recalculated from a frozen one-percent canary because model availability, tokenization, and prices can change.
